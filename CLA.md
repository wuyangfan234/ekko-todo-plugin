## Why this exists

Ekko ToDo is MIT-licensed. That does not change: every release we publish under MIT stays available
under MIT, for anyone, permanently.

What MIT alone cannot do is let the project *choose* its terms later. Once your patch is merged under
MIT, you still own your part of it, so the project could never offer a differently-licensed edition —
a source-available one, a commercial one — without tracking down every contributor for permission.

This agreement keeps both options open. It is the same thing Apache, Google and most projects of this
size ask for. **It does not take your copyright.**

## 8. How to sign

### 8.1 Individual

Add this exact line as a comment on your first pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

One comment covers every Contribution You make to the Project afterwards, under this or a later
version of the agreement.

### 8.2 Corporate

If Your Contribution is made in the course of work for an entity, an authorised representative of that
entity comments on the pull request with:

```
<Entity legal name> hereby signs the Ekko ToDo CLA v1.0.
Authorised signatory: <name, title>
Covered contributors: <GitHub handles or "all employees">
```

The entity then covers the listed people, and this section replaces §6.2 for them.

### 8.3 The record

The pull request comment **is** the signature: it is attributable, timestamped, and kept with the
repository's history. We may later move signature tracking to a bot or a separate file; signatures
collected this way under this version remain valid.

## 9. Scope and versions

This agreement applies to Contributions submitted on or after the effective date above. Contributions
merged before it are **not** retroactively covered — the Project has no claim on them beyond what MIT
already grants.

If We publish a later version, it will carry a higher number and a new effective date, and it will
apply only to Contributions submitted after *that* date. A version You signed stays valid for what You
submitted under it.

## 10. Misc
